Our story

How StyleHub Began

StyleHub started in a two-room studio with a simple frustration: beautifully made clothing was priced out of reach, and affordable clothing rarely lasted a season. We set out to close that gap.

Today we design in-house, work directly with a small circle of ateliers and skip the traditional retail mark-up — so a hand-finished blazer or a silk-touch dress costs what it should, not what a department store decides.

Responsibility

Sustainability at StyleHub

  • Sustainable materials — organic cotton, recycled polyester and certified viscose across 70% of the range.
  • Responsible manufacturing — audited partner ateliers with fair wages and safe conditions.
  • Reduced packaging — plastic-free mailers made from 90% recycled paper.
  • Ethical fashion — small-batch production to cut overstock and waste.
